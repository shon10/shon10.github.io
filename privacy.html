Privacy Policy

Brain Traps Uploader · Last updated 28 October 2026

Brain Traps Uploader is a private tool that the owner of the Brain Traps channel uses to manage their own YouTube channel. It has one user: the channel owner.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool can:

  • upload and schedule videos;
  • post and reply to comments on the channel's videos;
  • read the channel's own video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports.

It does not access the data of any other Google account.

How the data is used and stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer:

  • the sign-in token;
  • a log of scheduled uploads;
  • copies of public comments, used to reply to viewers;
  • the channel's own statistics.

None of it is sold or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Viewers

The tool only reads comments that viewers have posted publicly on Brain Traps videos. It uses them solely to reply on the channel. It does not collect any other information about viewers.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the files on the owner's computer then removes everything the tool stored.

Contact

For questions, reach Brain Traps through the YouTube channel or Instagram.
