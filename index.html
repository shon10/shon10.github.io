Brain Traps Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Brain Traps channel.

Brain Traps makes short brain-test videos: optical illusions, attention tests and quick puzzles.

Brain Traps Uploader is the small tool the channel owner uses to manage that work on their own YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules new videos, posts and replies to comments on them, and reads the channel's own view and audience statistics. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.
